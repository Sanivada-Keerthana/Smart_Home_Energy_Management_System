Smart Residential Energy Management System

A unified platform to monitor, control, and optimize energy usage across individual flats and shared community spaces.

Welcome, User

Role: ROLE

Flat: 101

Block: A

You are the system administrator. You can manage blocks, flats, approve users, monitor total apartment energy usage, and oversee the entire residential infrastructure.

You have full control over your flat’s devices, energy usage, automation rules, and approvals for family members and guests.

You can control assigned devices, view energy consumption, and participate in automation features within your flat.

You have view-only access to devices and energy data for the flat you are visiting.

You manage common-area devices like lifts, parking lights, gym equipment, and oversee shared energy usage across the block.

You can monitor and operate assigned common-area devices to ensure safety and operational efficiency.
